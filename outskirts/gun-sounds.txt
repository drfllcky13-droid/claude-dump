License: CC0 = public domain, no credit needed. BY = free, but it must be credited in the game's credits.

Listen to each one before using it: some are phone recordings and will need cleanup.

SUPPRESSOR RULE (from how real guns work)
  Suppressors make a gun quieter, not silent. A bullet faster than sound still cracks.
  .22 and .45 bullets are slower than sound, so suppressed they're the quietest guns in the game.
  Revolvers can't take one (the gap at the cylinder leaks the blast). Neither can launchers, grenades or the flare gun.
  Suggested suppressor kinds (generic names, rename freely): pistol can (9 mm/.45), .22 can, rifle can, shotgun can, .50 can.

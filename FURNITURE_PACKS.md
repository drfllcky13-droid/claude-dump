# Finward's ecosystem anchors a believable American suburb

*Data gathered from the live Unity Asset Store on 2026-10-03. Prices are list prices on that date. A sitewide "Autumn Sale 2026" banner was live, so prices may move.*

**Finward Studios is the only publisher that combines four things: realistic first-person PBR, a deliberately suburban American theme, Unity 6 URP compatibility listed on the store, and a real review base.** Its **House Props & Furniture Vol. 2** ($79, 5 stars from 11 ratings), **Suburb Neighborhood House Pack** ($89, 5 stars from 127 ratings) and **Atmospheric House** ($99, 5 stars from 58 ratings) take the top three places. All three list 6000.x with Built-in, URP and HDRP, and two of them were updated in the last two weeks of September 2026 ([Suburb Neighborhood](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/urban/suburb-neighborhood-house-pack-modular-72712), [House Props Vol. 2](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/interior/house-props-furniture-vol-2-265523)).

No single pack covers all nine of your categories (kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room, garage, laundry, clutter, food packaging, appliances). Specialist packs fill the gaps:

- **32cm** for American fridges, washers and garage shelving.
- **VIS Games** and **CoolWorks** for bathrooms.
- **Phoenix3D** and **3D Division** for close-up canned food.
- **Robot Skeleton** for the only washer-and-dryer pair found.

A full kit that covers every room costs about **$358 at list price**.

Almost every specialist pack has zero to two ratings, so most quality judgements rest on store specs, not user feedback. Only two relevant packs carry a "Created with AI" disclosure, and neither is in the core recommendation. LODs are the weakest point across this whole category: few packs state full LOD chains.

**Why this order.** The Finward packs win on the combination that matters most for a shipping Unity 6 URP game: verified 6000.x support, recent updates, and the only meaningful review volume in the category. Users describe Suburb Neighborhood furniture as looking realistic "even in URP". That comes from a search summary of its 95 reviews and was not read in full ([reviews](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/urban/suburb-neighborhood-house-pack-modular-72712/reviews)).

Each lower-ranked whole-house pack has one serious weakness:

- **NOT_Lonely (#4)** has the strongest reputation for an explicitly American house (116 ratings, tagged "usa", "FPS", "Horror"), but lists no Unity 6 version ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/urban/hq-residential-house-48976)).

Places 8–10 are specialists. Each fills a category the whole-house packs leave thin: American appliances, close-up food, and garage shelving.

Several packs just missed the top 10. **Dirty Apartments** (Triplebrick, $35, 4★ from 55 ratings, 6000.0.29f1 URP) is the best-reviewed post-apocalyptic interior kit, but it is built for apartments with 200–1,200-tri props ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/urban/dirty-apartments-21403)). **Dirty Bathroom Collection – HQ** (VIS Games, $14.95, 6000.0.24 URP, 4K, clean/dirty/bloody variants) is the best themed bathroom, but it has only seven fixtures ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/dirty-bathroom-collection-hq-333160)). ## Best value under $100: House Props & Furniture Vol. 2 at $79

**Best value under $100: Finward Studios' House Props & Furniture Vol. 2 ($79).**

It is the densest realistic prop library that is current on Unity 6 URP:

- **356 meshes and 177 textures**, mostly 2K and 4K, with slight built-in wear that suits a post-outbreak suburb.
- Openable cabinets, drawers and doors with modeled interiors, which are natural loot containers.
- Tested on **6000.0.83f1 across all three pipelines**, updated 2026-09-26.
- Every one of its 11 reviews is five stars ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/interior/house-props-furniture-vol-2-265523), [reviews](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/interior/house-props-furniture-vol-2-265523/reviews)).
- It is marketed as an add-on to the Suburb Neighborhood and Atmospheric House shells, so it fits the same art direction if you buy a house kit later.

Two caveats temper the pick. First, the store page gives counts but **no itemized contents**, so its coverage of toilets, tubs, washers and beds is **unverified**. Second, LODs exist only on "a few most triangle heavy objects".

If the budget is tighter, **House Furniture Pack ($35)** from the same studio is the runner-up. It has 170+ props, 5★ from 27 ratings, and the same 6000.0.83f1 listing, but its textures top out at 2K, which is weaker for first-person close-ups ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/house-furniture-pack-88646)). If you need house shells rather than props, Suburb Neighborhood ($89) is the better sub-$100 buy, because it includes furnished houses and the street.

## Only two packs carry a "Created with AI" notice

The store's AI disclosure lives in a product field called `aiDescription`. It was **empty for every pack in the Top 10, the buy list and the honorable mentions**. Across the four research streams, that covers well over 100 checked listings. Only two relevant packs had the field filled in.

**Laundry and Functional Washing Machine Pack** (Rip Vertices Studio, $19.99, URP 2022.3) discloses that "the WashingMachineController script… was developed with the support of AI-based assistance". The disclosure covers code, not art ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/clothing/laundry-and-functional-washing-machine-pack-318699)).

**American Suburb Top-Down Pack** ($14 on sale from $34.99, 5★ from 17 ratings, 6000.0.74) discloses that "some high-poly source models and texture elements were created with the assistance of AI tools", and that its scripts were AI-assisted too ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/slug/395606)). It is thematically on target, but it is built for top-down cameras: props are 4–850 polys, textures are 2K and there are no LODs. It will not hold up in first person. The research notes disagree on its publisher: one names Art Equilibrium and another names "Abstract". Confirm on the store page.

In the other direction, 3D Division Studios advertises "handcrafted, AI-free textures and logos" on its canned-food packs ([store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/food/canned-food-fruits-and-vegetables-pbr-game-ready-401732)).

Two limits apply to all of this:

- The visible badge was not seen on a rendered page. Its presence or absence is inferred from the data field.
- An empty field means the publisher declared no AI use. It is not independent proof.

## Caveats: thin ratings, unverified fields, screenshot checks

**Thin ratings.** Outside Finward (11–127 ratings per pack), NOT_Lonely (116), Gabro Media (14) and Dirty Apartments (55), nearly every recommended pack has **0–2 ratings**. The store hides the star average below about three ratings. Places 7–10 of the Top 10, and every bathroom, laundry, appliance and food pick, are therefore judged on publisher specs and screenshots alone. No Reddit or Unity forum quality comparisons were found for any of them.

**Unverified fields.** The following data points could not be confirmed:

- **Prices:**
  - Willard Artson's living-room pack, Polyvers Living Room Furniture, Must Have Studio's Realistic House Interior & Exterior and The Naked Dev's Abandoned House all returned no price. They may be unpurchasable.
  - Gabro Media shows $36 in the store data but a "40% off $60 until Oct 12" claim in its description.
  - Basement House Props rendered once at $16.50 against a $15 JSON price.
- **Unity 6 support:** these lists show only what each publisher submitted for testing. No pack was actually imported into Unity 6 URP during this research.
- **Contents:** Finward Vol. 2 has no itemized list. Bendylab's appliance list comes from a search snippet. The contents of the Dekogon kitchen volumes are unknown.
- **LODs:** unstated for all Finward shells, NOT_Lonely, every 32cm pack, Bendylab, Kraffing and the garage packs.

**Screenshot checks for a US look.** Before buying, check the screenshots for these details:

- **Finward:** a Finnish studio. Its suburban porches, mailboxes and electric poles read as North American, but check outlets, switches and appliance styling for Nordic cues.
- **32cm "American fridges":** confirm the French-door or top-freezer styling.
- **Washers:** front-loaders read as European. The 80s top-loaders may look more authentically suburban American.
- **Bathroom packs:** look for close-coupled toilets, alcove tubs and US vanities. Avoid the Arigasoft bidet and the Nexus squat toilet.
- **Bedroom packs:** POLYBOX and ArchVizPRO are "minimal & modern" or Scandinavian.
- **Food and appliance packs:** check that labels are fictional rather than real trademarks. Dekogon and 3D Division state their labels are custom; most others are silent.

**Performance.** The Finward demo scenes run **0.94–1.5M tris** with LODs not stated (Atmospheric House is about 1.1M). Several 32cm and Kraffing appliances exceed 40k tris. A streamed suburb with many enterable houses will need occlusion culling, room-by-room loading, 2K texture import caps and generated LODs on top of anything these packs ship.

## Conclusion

The Unity Store's realistic-interior market splits into two tiers. One is a small, well-maintained tier of whole-house kits, led by Finward and followed by NOT_Lonely and Gabro Media. The other is a long tail of 2025–26 specialist packs that are often technically strong (4K, 3 LODs, Unity 6 URP) but have almost no reviews. For this game, the practical strategy is to lock the art direction on Finward first, then treat every specialist purchase as provisional until it passes a test import into a Unity 6 URP scene beside Finward assets. At $5–$30 per specialist pack, buying, testing and discarding a miss costs less than over-researching.

The real risk is not missing furniture. It is the American-specific hero loot the store does not sell in current URP form: the slide-in range, the dryer, the cereal box and the cluttered junk drawer. Those few items get the most first-person screen time in a suburban survival game. Plan to budget custom modelling or Fab-sourced assets for them from the start, rather than hoping a later store purchase will close the gap.

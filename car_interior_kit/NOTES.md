# car_interior_kit — The Outskirts driving view

All layers 640x360 PNG, real alpha, hard pixels, same origin. Show at 2x/3x nearest-neighbour.
Everything is generated by `make_kit.py` (Pillow only); edit numbers there and rerun to regenerate.

## Stacking order (bottom to top)

1. game world (3D, rendered behind)
2. `mod_plow` — plow blade top edge, outside the glass
3. `glass_<window>_<stage>` — windscreen / left / right, chipped / cracked / broken (pick one stage per window, or none)
4. `mod_bars` — welded bars, inside the glass
5. `interior_frame` — roof, pillars, visors, mirror, door tops, dash, centre stack, closed glovebox, seats, clutter. Windows fully transparent.
6. `glovebox_open` — optional, covers the closed glovebox area and hangs over the seat
7. `dash_lights` — dial faces, ticks, needles, radio display
8. `lamp_fuel`, `lamp_engine`, `lamp_highbeam` — optional, on-state only
9. `wheel` — steering wheel alone
10. `driver` — head, shoulders, arms, hands (above the wheel because the hands grip the rim from the camera side)
11. `night_tint` — optional, darkens the interior only; has holes over the dials, lamps and radio display with a dithered halo so they stay readable

Fallback for turning: swap layers 9+10 for a single `driver_left` or `driver_right` (body + arms + wheel drawn turned about 30 degrees).

## Wheel

Pivot: **(245, 268)** in the 640x360 canvas. The rim is an ellipse (68 x 40 px radii) because it is seen from behind and above,
so an in-engine rotation will distort it past about 20 degrees. That is what `driver_left` / `driver_right` are for.

## Readout-safe areas (kept flat and dark)

top centre: roof band, y 0-36. bottom centre and bottom right: flat seat cloth below y 320.

## Unsure about

- Night tint strength (alpha 150). If the game also dims the world, you may want it lighter; it is one number in `night_tint()`.
- Dash lights are drawn lit at all times. If you want them off by day, drop layer 7; the frame has dark dial faces underneath.
- Broken-glass shards are 150-255 alpha pale blue-white; over a bright daytime world they may want darkening.
- The driver's left arm is foreshortened a lot (camera is over his right shoulder); if it reads wrong, the arm anchors are in `driver()`.
- Camera is slightly left of centre so the passenger headrest sits lower-right and the glovebox is clear of it.
